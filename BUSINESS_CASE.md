## ** X ParkWindow Value Proposition and Why I Created It 
X ParkWindow is a parking planner created for STFX students who drive to campus for class and/or to study. Someone that commutes everyday needs to find out where they can park, how long it'll take to walk to class from their spot, where empty lots are and how early they must leave their house to get to class on time. I decided to create this because on rainy days, even though i live on campus I drive to my classes. On these days i can never find a spot near the buildings my classes are in and I end up being late to my classes. This app would've helped me find a spot quicker, leave my apartment at a reasonable time dependent on spots available and where I could've parked.
