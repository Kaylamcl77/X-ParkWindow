## The Idea
X ParkWindow is designed for STFX students who commute to campus for class. A student can know exactly when their classes start but not know when they should leave home, and where to park. Students also need time between finding an open spot that their permit allows them to park at and walk from their car to class. A commuter might solve this issue by leaving extra early, trying a lot they're familiarized with, and hoping there is a spot open and if not, driving and trying anther lot if it's full. The trade-off between leaving more time than they need and risking a late arrival to class.  

X ParkWindow helps students make those decisions before even leaving their house. All it takes is entering their permit type, class or study location, start and end times, estimated drive time, and their walking pace. The planner compares lots and displays an estimated availability window, permit cutoffs, walking time, and suggested departure time. This gives students a first choice and backup they can compare ahead of time. 

The proposed revenue model is an annual subscription fee paid by STFX, which later on is charged to the student accounts. This allows students to have "free" access to this tool. The university would receive a commuter planning tool and in a production version a dashboard showing when and where demand is higher. This could be beneficial by showing STFX where more or less parking areas need to be added to satisfy the community and facilitate Safety and Securities constant patrol for parking tickets. A pilot could test whether students spend less time looking for parking and whether STFX find the demand high enough to justify paying for the service.
