## The Idea
X ParkWindow is designed for STFX students who commute to campus for class. A student can know exactly when their classes start but not know when to leave home and where to park. Students also need time between finding an open spot that their permit allows and to walk from their car to class. Commuters might solve this issue by leaving extra early, trying lots they're familiarized with, and hoping there's a spot open and trying another lot if it's full. The trade-off between leaving more time than they need and risking late arrival to classes. 

X ParkWindow helps students make the decision of where to park and when to leave before leaving their house. It takes entering their permit type, class location, start and end times, estimated drive time, and walking pace. The planner compares lots and displays an estimated availability window, permit cutoffs, walking time, and suggested departure time. This gives students first choice and backup they can compare ahead of time. 

An annual subscription fee paid by STFX could be a way X ParkWindow generates revenue. The university would receive a planning tool and in a future version with a dashboard showing which parking lots are busiest and at what times. STFX could use the dashboard to see which lots are the busiest and at what times. They can then use that information when deciding whether to add parking. A pilot could test whether students spend less time looking for parking and whether STFX finds the demand high enough to justify paying for the service.
