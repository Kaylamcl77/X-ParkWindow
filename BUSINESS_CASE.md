## X ParkWindow Value Proposition and Why I Created It
X ParkWindow is a parking planner created for STFX students who drive to campus for class and/or to study. Someone that commutes everyday needs to find out where they can park, how long it'll take to walk to class from their spot, where empty lots are and how early they must leave their house to get to class on time. I decided to create this because on rainy days, even though i live on campus I drive to my classes. On these days i can never find a spot near the buildings my classes are in and I end up being late to my classes. 

This prototype could help commuters with their decisions all in a few clicks. A student only needs to enter their parking permit type, how long it takes to drive to class and select where they want to go from the time their class starts until their class ends or whenever they are done studying on campus. This app also can connect to your health app to analyze your walking trends and speed which allows the app to calculate the walking distance from where you plan to park and your walking speed to accurately get a estimated arrival time to your class.
